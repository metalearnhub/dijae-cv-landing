How to post

  1. Tap Copy post and paste it into LinkedIn.
  2. Tap Download image and attach it. Paste the alt text into LinkedIn's alt text field.
  3. Publish, then tap Copy first comment and post it as your first comment.
  4. On the post, open the … menu → Copy link to post. Paste it in the card's Verify box.
  5. Reply to every comment within the first hour.

About verification

Verify checks that the link is a real LinkedIn post, reads its publish time from the post ID, confirms it's from your profile and isn't reused on another card. It can't read the post's text, so use Show post preview to confirm. Verifications are saved in this browser only.
